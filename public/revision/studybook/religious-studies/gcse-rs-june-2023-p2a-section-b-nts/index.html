AQA GCSE RS June 2023 — Thematic Studies (Paper 2A)

June 2023 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2A Section B Perspectives On Faith Non Textual Studies

Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following is not a teaching in ‘The Theology of the Body’? A Couples should not have more children than they can provide for. B Men and women show their love for each other through sex. C People discover their dignity through the body. D Using artificial contraception strengthens a couple’s relationship.

Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two ways in which Christian parents can make a loving home for their children.

Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views on homosexuality. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Catholic beliefs about gender discrimination. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘The Catholic marriage vows (promises) are outdated.’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you: • should give reasoned arguments in support of this statement • should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • should refer to Christian arguments • may refer to non-religious arguments • should reach a justified conclusion.

Q2.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following gives the meaning of the term ‘conflict resolution’? A Creating peace between enemies. B Encouraging enemies to go to war. C Making the two sides angry with each other. D Supplying both sides in a conflict with the same amount of weapons.

Q2.2 [2 marks]

Give two reasons why Christians believe that terrorism is wrong.

Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting Old Testament teachings about going to war (armed conflict). • You must refer to a Christian teaching. • Your contrasting teaching may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Christian views about anger as a response to injustice. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘The consequences (results) of going to war are never good.’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you: • should give reasoned arguments in support of this statement • should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • should refer to Christian arguments • may refer to non-religious arguments • should reach a justified conclusion.

Q3.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following means taking unfair advantage of the poor? A Exploitation B Justice C Protection D Responsibility

Q3.2 [2 marks]

Give two causes of poverty.

Q3.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views about stewardship of wealth. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q3.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Christian beliefs about justice. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q3.5 [12 marks]

‘If everyone obeyed Christian teachings, there would be no need for laws about human rights.’ Evaluate this statement.
