AQA GCSE RS June 2024 — Judaism Perspectives (Paper 2Y)

June 2024 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2Y Section A Perspectives On Faith Judaism

Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following does not refer to the sanctity of human life? A Human life is holy. B Human life is meaningless. C Human life is special. D Human life is valuable.

Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two commandments about how Jews should treat others.

Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting Jewish beliefs about the Messiah.

Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Jewish beliefs about the covenant between God and Abraham. Refer to scripture or another source of Jewish belief and teaching in your answer.

Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘For Jews, belief in God as Creator gives the best understanding of the nature of God.’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you should: • give reasoned arguments to support this statement • give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • refer to Jewish teaching • reach a justified conclusion. [SPaG 3 marks]

Q2.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following practices should Jews carry out on Yom Kippur? A Eat apples and honey B Fast for twenty-five hours C Eat bitter herbs D Leave an empty chair for Elijah

Q2.2 [2 marks]

Name two religious features that are found in Orthodox synagogues.

Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two ways in which Jewish mourning rituals (customs) influence the lives of families who have lost a loved one.

Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Jewish beliefs about the importance of a girl’s Bat Mitzvah ceremony. Refer to scripture or another source of Jewish belief and teaching in your answer.

Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘Spending time with family is the main purpose of Shabbat (the Jewish Sabbath).’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you should: • give reasoned arguments to support this statement • give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • refer to Jewish teaching • reach a justified conclusion.
