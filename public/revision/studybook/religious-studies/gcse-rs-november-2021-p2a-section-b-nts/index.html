AQA GCSE RS June 2021 — Thematic Studies (Paper 2A)

June 2021 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2A Section B Perspectives On Faith Non Textual Studies

Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following refers to methods that some Christians might use to prevent pregnancy? A Adultery B Contraception C Procreation D Cohabitation

Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two reasons why Christians might believe that gender discrimination is wrong.

Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views in contemporary British society about the role of women in the home. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Catholic beliefs about the nature and purpose of sexual love. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘Same-sex couples should be able to get married in a Catholic church.’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you: • should give reasoned arguments in support of this statement • should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • should refer to Christian arguments • may refer to non-religious arguments • should reach a justified conclusion.

Q2.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following means to mend broken relationships? A Pacifism B Radicalisation C Justice D Reconciliation

Q2.2 [2 marks]

Name two Christian organisations that are working for peace.

Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views in contemporary British society about using nuclear weapons in war. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two ways in which Christians may support those involved in 21st century wars. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘Non-violent resistance is the right response to injustice.’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you: • should give reasoned arguments in support of this statement • should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • should refer to Christian arguments • may refer to non-religious arguments • should reach a justified conclusion.

Q3.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following means that Christians show respect for the different views of others? A Responsibility B Dignity C Tolerance D Stewardship

Q3.2 [2 marks]

Give two ways in which Christians might support victims of racial prejudice.

Q3.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views in contemporary British society about the work of Christian organisations that try to end poverty. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q3.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Christian beliefs about why it is important for everyone to have human rights. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q3.5 [12 marks]

‘It is always good to be wealthy.’ Evaluate this statement.
