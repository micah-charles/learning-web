AQA GCSE RS June 2024 — Thematic Studies (Paper 2A)

June 2024 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2A Section B Perspectives On Faith Non Textual Studies

Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following is the Catholic Church’s declaration that a marriage is not valid? A Annulment B Cohabitation C Divorce D Separation

Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two Christian teachings which show that women and men are equal.

Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views in contemporary British society about remarriage after divorce. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Christian beliefs about sex before marriage. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘Procreation (having children) is an essential part of being a Catholic family.’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you: • should give reasoned arguments to support this statement • should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • should refer to Christian arguments • may refer to non-religious arguments • should reach a justified conclusion.

Q2.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following explains what is meant by nuclear deterrence? A Getting rid of all nuclear weapons B Possessing nuclear weapons to prevent attack C Telling other countries to develop nuclear weapons D Using nuclear weapons to destroy enemy cities

Q2.2 [2 marks]

Give two teachings from the Catechism of the Catholic Church 2309 about Just War theory.

Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views in contemporary British society about forgiveness. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Christian beliefs about the use of torture. Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘Religion is never a good reason for going to war.’ Evaluate this statement. In your answer you: • should give reasoned arguments to support this statement • should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view • should refer to Christian arguments • may refer to non-religious arguments • should reach a justified conclusion.

Q3.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following terms means ‘treating people fairly’? A Dignity B Justice C Responsibility D Stewardship

Q3.2 [2 marks]

Give two reasons why Christians disagree with discrimination based on sexuality.

Q3.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views in contemporary British society about the Christian teaching to ‘love your neighbour’. • You must refer to a Christian view. • Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or non-religious tradition.

Q3.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Christian beliefs about being wealthy (rich). Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

Q3.5 [12 marks]

‘The “Preferential Option for the Poor” is not fair.’ Evaluate this statement.
