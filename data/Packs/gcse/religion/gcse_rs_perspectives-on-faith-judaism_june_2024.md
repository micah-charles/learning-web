• Bat Mitzvah means ‘daughter of the commandment’ / the girl now takes on her role as an adult
member of the Jewish community / learning how to keep a Jewish home / eg lighting the Shabbat
candles / food laws / the use of the mikvah, etc.

the Torah / gives a short speech / and may lead part of the synagogue service / she now has a role
to play in public worship / able to be part of the minyan, etc.
