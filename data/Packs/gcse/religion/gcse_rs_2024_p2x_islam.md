# AQA GCSE RS 2024 — Perspectives on Faith: Islam (Paper 2X)

*June 2024 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2X Section A*

---

## Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following is not one of the five roots of Usul ad-Din in Shi’a Islam?
A Justice
B Prophethood
C Holy books
D The oneness of God

## Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two reasons why Muhammad is important for Muslims.

## Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two ways in which a belief in angels influences the lives of Muslims.

## Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two reasons why Muslims believe that the Qur’an has authority.
Refer to scripture or another source of Muslim belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘Muslim beliefs about hell make it difficult to believe that God is merciful.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Muslim teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
[SPaG 3 marks]

## Q2.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following celebrates the end of Ramadan?
A Ashura
B Id-ul-Adha
C Jummah
D Id-ul-Fitr

## Q2.2 [2 marks]

Give two religious actions that Muslims perform during hajj.

## Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting Muslim views about the importance of prayer.

## Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Muslim beliefs about the benefits of fasting (sawm).
Refer to scripture or another source of Muslim belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘Zakah (giving alms) is the best way for Muslims to show their obedience to God.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Muslim teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
