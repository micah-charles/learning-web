*June 2021 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2A Section B Perspectives On Faith Non Textual Studies*

## Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following refers to methods that some Christians might use to
prevent pregnancy?
A Adultery
B Contraception
C Procreation
D Cohabitation

## Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two reasons why Christians might believe that gender discrimination is wrong.

## Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting views in contemporary British society about the role of
women in the home.
• You must refer to a Christian view.
• Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or
non-religious tradition.

## Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Catholic beliefs about the nature and purpose of sexual love.
Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘Same-sex couples should be able to get married in a Catholic church.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you:
• should give reasoned arguments in support of this statement
• should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• should refer to Christian arguments
• may refer to non-religious arguments
• should reach a justified conclusion.
