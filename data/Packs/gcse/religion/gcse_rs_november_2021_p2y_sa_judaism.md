# AQA GCSE RS June 2021 — Judaism Perspectives (Paper 2Y)

*June 2021 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2Y Section A Perspectives On Faith Judaism*

---

## Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following gives the meaning of ‘Pikuach Nefesh’?
A Kindness to others
B Charity
C Healing the world
D Saving life

## Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two Jewish beliefs about the nature of God.

## Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two ways in which belief in free will influences the lives of Jews.

## Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Jewish beliefs about resurrection.
Refer to scripture or another source of Jewish belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘It is impossible for Jews today to keep all the Ten Commandments.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Jewish teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
[SPaG 3 marks]

## Q2.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following is the standing prayer?
A Bimah
B Amidah
C Talmud
D Trefah

## Q2.2 [2 marks]

Give two ways in which many Jews celebrate Shabbat in the home.

## Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting Jewish views about Jewish dietary (food) laws.

## Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two reasons why Pesach is important for Jews in Great Britain today.
Refer to scripture or another source of Jewish belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘The Brit Milah (circumcision) ceremony is more important for Jews than the
Bar Mitzvah ceremony.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Jewish teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
