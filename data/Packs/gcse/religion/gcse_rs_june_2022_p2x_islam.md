# AQA GCSE RS June 2022 — Islam Perspectives (Paper 2X)

*June 2022 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2X Perspectives On Faith Islam*

---

## Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following holy books was revealed to Jesus (Isa) according to
Muslim belief?
A The Gospel
B The Psalms
C The Qur’an
D The Torah

## Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two of the Six Articles of Faith in Sunni Islam.

## Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two ways in which the religious beliefs about God as Creator are similar in
Islam and in the main religious tradition of Great Britain.
You should name the main religious tradition of Great Britain in your answer.

## Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Muslim beliefs about the importance of Ibrahim (Abraham).
Refer to scripture or another source of Muslim belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘Belief in the Imamate is the most important of the Five Roots of Usul-ad-Din
(principles of faith) in Shi’a Islam.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Muslim teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
[SPaG 3 marks]

## Q2.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following words is the Muslim term for fasting?
A Hajj
B Salah
C Khums
D Sawm

## Q2.2 [2 marks]

Give the two parts of the Shahadah (the Muslim declaration of faith).

## Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two ways in which celebrating Id-ul-Fitr influences the lives of Muslims.

## Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two ways in which the Night of Power (Laylat ul-Qadr) is important
for Muslims.
Refer to scripture or another source of Muslim belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘All Muslims should go to a mosque each week for Friday prayer (Jummah).’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Muslim teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
[SPaG 3 marks]
