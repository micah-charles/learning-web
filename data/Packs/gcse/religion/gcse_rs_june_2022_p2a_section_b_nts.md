## Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following is needed for a couple’s marriage to be valid according to
the teaching of the Catholic Church?
A Being closely related to each other.
B Getting married of their own free choice.
C Intending to use contraception until they feel ready to have children.
D Marrying to please their parents.

## Q1.2 [2 marks]

be parents.

## Q1.3 [4 marks]

• You must refer to a Christian belief.
• Your contrasting belief may come from Christianity or from another religious or
non-religious tradition.

## Q1.4 [5 marks]

Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘Biblical teaching supports the view that women and men are equal.’

• should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• should refer to Christian arguments

• should reach a justified conclusion.
