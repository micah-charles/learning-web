# AQA GCSE RS June 2023 — Judaism Perspectives (Paper 2Y)

*June 2023 — AQA GCSE Religious Studies — Paper 2Y Section A Perspectives On Faith Judaism*

---

## Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following is a Jewish belief about life after death?
A Mitzvot
B Resurrection
C Monotheism
D Shekhinah

## Q1.2 [2 marks]

Give two ways in which Jews carry out ‘healing the world’.

## Q1.3 [4 marks]

Explain two contrasting beliefs about the Messiah held by Judaism and the main
religious tradition of Great Britain.
You should name the main religious tradition of Great Britain in your answer.

## Q1.4 [5 marks]

Explain two Jewish beliefs about God as Judge.
Refer to scripture or another source of Jewish belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘For Jews, the promised land is the most important promise in the
Covenant with Abraham.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Jewish teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
[SPaG 3 marks]

## Q2.1 [1 mark]

At which one of the following ceremonies does a Jewish man often smash a glass
under his foot?
A Bar Mitzvah
B Funeral
C Brit Milah
D Marriage

## Q2.2 [2 marks]

Give two of the foods that have symbolic (special) meaning at Pesach.

## Q2.3 [4 marks]

Explain two ways in which private prayer influences the lives of Jews.

## Q2.4 [5 marks]

Explain two ways in which Jews make sure that food is kosher (meets the
requirements of Jewish law).
Refer to scripture or another source of Jewish belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q2.5 [12 marks]

‘For Jews, the similarities between synagogue services (public acts of worship) in
Orthodox and Reform synagogues are more important than the differences.’
Evaluate this statement.
In your answer you should:
• give reasoned arguments to support this statement
• give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• refer to Jewish teaching
• reach a justified conclusion.
[SPaG 3 marks]
