## Q1.1 [1 mark]

Which one of the following is not a teaching in ‘The Theology of the Body’?
A Couples should not have more children than they can provide for.
B Men and women show their love for each other through sex.
C People discover their dignity through the body.
D Using artificial contraception strengthens a couple’s relationship.

## Q1.2 [2 marks]

## Q1.3 [4 marks]

• You must refer to a Christian view.
• Your contrasting view may come from Christianity or from another religious or
non-religious tradition.

## Q1.4 [5 marks]

Refer to scripture or another source of Christian belief and teaching in your answer.

## Q1.5 [12 marks]

‘The Catholic marriage vows (promises) are outdated.’

• should give reasoned arguments to support a different point of view
• should refer to Christian arguments
• may refer to non-religious arguments
• should reach a justified conclusion.
